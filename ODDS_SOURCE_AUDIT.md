# Recommendation: Consolidate to The Odds API — Cancel SharpAPI

## 1. Headline

**Cancel SharpAPI. Keep your current TOA plan exactly as-is. Saves ~$250/mo (~$3,000/yr, 50% of odds spend).**

The numbers: live headers confirm the plan size exactly (21,903,959 remaining + 3,096,041 used = 25,000,000). Burn is ~320K credits/day (~9.7M/mo) — but that extrapolation assumes the current billing cycle is ~9–10 days in. **Pre-cancel action item #1: confirm the billing-cycle anchor date on the TOA invoice before trusting 320K/day** (error is in the safe direction — if 3.1M accumulated over longer, real burn is lower). Absorbing all SharpAPI-primary sports at SharpAPI-equivalent freshness adds ~1.9–2M/mo with the fast-loop cadences specified in §4 → ~11.5–12M/mo June, projected 17–23M at winter peak (NBA+NHL+NCAAB+NFL). **Pre-cancel action item #2: re-run the winter projection after week 0 locks the soccer-key cadence** (generic "soccer" is one SharpAPI call today but expands to ~14 active TOA keys now, historically ~53 in club season — uncurated, that alone could add 3–8M/mo; curated per §4, it adds <1M). That fits the 25M plan with headroom. **Do NOT downgrade the TOA tier** — the public 15M/$249 tier is marginal at winter peak. If you're paying more than $249/mo for the 25M plan, that's a separate negotiation, but don't trade down; winter will breach 15M.

What you give up (eyes open):
- **MLB probable pitchers / NHL goalies** — the only hard gap; needs ~1 day of new code (MLB Stats API is free, no key). Until built: no 3-min lineup-change decline guard, no K-prop+ML same-team SGP carve-out.
- **Tennis overnight coverage** when TOA lags new tournaments (~7am ET gap) — these are **hard-dark decline windows, full stop**. The DK game-line supplement is fill-only (it can only enrich events already in the parsed cache, odds-feed.js:1087-1130) — when TOA returns 0 events there is nothing to fill, so the scraper cannot backstop this. Accept the windows; event-creation-from-scraper is a possible later build, not part of this migration.
- **MLB F5 line depth on thin games** — the repo's own comment (odds-feed.js:962-978) documents that TOA's per-event F5 response often carries only Pinnacle's pick-em (line=0) on thin-volume games while SharpAPI supplied BetMGM at the standard ±0.5; the byLine union loses Sharp's side. Mitigation in week 0: widen the F5 per-event supplement's bookmaker list (free — TOA bills per-10 books) and probe whether TOA offers alternate F5 line markets to add to the supplement. Residual thin-game ±0.5 declines are accepted.
- **Live in-play fair-prob tracking for soccer and MMA** — refreshLiveOdds (order-tracker.js:4094) covers only NBA/MLB/NHL/NFL via TOA (LIVE_ODDS_API_SPORTS, :4139); other LEAGUE_MAP sports hit SharpAPI live today. Post-cancel, in-progress soccer/MMA legs lose liveFairProb. Display-only (dashboard live P&L), not pricing — accept the loss and gate refreshLiveOdds to skip those sports (week 0) so it doesn't burn calls on a dead path.
- **MMA small-card breadth + earlier fighter posting** — major UFC cards are full parity (Pin/DK/FD); long-tail promotions may fail 2-book de-vig → safe declines. DK scraper backstops full cards, **with the cache re-merge hook from §3.6** (without it, DK-only fights flap out of cache on every TOA refresh).
- **Closing-line snapshot freshness** for flipped sports degrades from ~30s-fresh (delta loop) to fast-loop cadence — CLV analytics noise, not pricing risk.
- **Kalshi reference rows, Caesars/BetMGM de-vig depth, Sharp shadow-prop telemetry** — de-vig depth is recoverable for free by widening the TOA bookmaker list (per-10 billing), but that is a pricing-input change and is sequenced AFTER the vig-filter port, in its own change window (§4) — not bundled into the cutover.

**Fallback if a blocker emerges** (shadow week shows TOA fair-prob drift, or the lineup feed proves unreliable in MLB season): keep SharpAPI but downgrade scope — it would then exist solely for lineups + generic soccer + tennis backstop. Ask SharpAPI for a cheaper tier covering only MLB/NHL/soccer; even a partial downgrade beats $250/mo for data that's 80% redundant. But based on the live parity probe (FULL parity on NBA/MLB+F5/NHL/WNBA with Pin/DK/FD today), I don't expect to need this.

## 3. Hidden Dependencies (and the fix for each)

1. **lineupCache (MLB pitchers / NHL goalies)** — fed EXCLUSIVELY by SharpAPI team-name parentheticals ("Yankees (Gerrit Cole)", odds-feed.js:571-577, 7305, 7333). No TOA equivalent. Consumers: pricer.js:851 (3-min lineup-swap decline grace — a real risk control), pricer.js:3349-3361 (`getPitcherSide` — K-prop+ML SGP carve-out hard-declines without it; fail-closed, lost volume not bad pricing). **Fix: MLB Stats API (free, keyless) feeding `updateLineupState`; NHL goalies via ESPN or accept the loss.** The single gating dependency — build first.
2. **Consensus-floor / vig-filter parity** — the flip changes pricing semantics, not just the data source. The `fetchPinnacleRows` merge path prices through buildConsensusMoneyline (≥0.50 Pinnacle favorite floor + filterSharpBooks high-vig filter); routing via ODDS_API_FALLBACK prices through fetchFromTheOddsApi's own parse (≥0.65 floor, no vig filter). Flipped naively, favorites between 50–65% silently lose floor protection — the exact Saba/soft-book-drag failure the 0.50 floor was added for. **Fix: port the 0.50 floor + filterSharpBooks into the TOA parse before any flip, and compute shadow fair probs through the production TOA parse, not a re-implemented average.**
3. **Supplement hoist covers FOUR things, not three** — F5, H1, team-totals, **and `_supplementDkGameLines`** (odds-feed.js:925-931, the operator-directive scraper backstop) are all Sharp-branch-only; fetchFromTheOddsApi ends with no supplements (:2046-2329). Flipping any sport without the full hoist silently kills the DK game-line backstop for exactly that sport. (Context: the DK supplement is fill-only, and its tennis + 11 club-soccer entries in DK_GAME_LINE_SPORTS are already dead code today for the same early-return reason — the hoist revives them only where a parsed cache exists.)
4. **MLB F5 byLine depth** — even after the hoist, cancelling Sharp removes its side of the `_mergeSupplementedMarket` union (odds-feed.js:962-978): thin-game F5 RFQs at standard ±0.5 will decline when TOA's per-event response is Pin-pick-em-only. **Fix: widen the F5 supplement's bookmaker list to 10 (free per-10 billing) + probe TOA alternate-F5 markets; accept the residual.**
5. **Club soccer keys** — two configured keys are dead against TOA today regardless of migration: `soccer_conmebol_libertadores` (TOA's live key is `soccer_conmebol_copa_libertadores` — remap exists for scores at :7447 and `_listEventsFromToa` at :4374-4376 but NOT the odds fetch at :266) and `soccer_usa_nwsl` (absent from `/sports?all=true`, live-verified). Fix both in week 0.
6. **MMA cache overwrite** — fetchFromTheOddsApi overwrites `oddsCache[sport]` wholesale (:2323), wiping `mergeDkMmaFights`-created DK-only fights every refresh; today the 30s post-delta re-merge (:6882-6893) re-adds them, and that disappears with the delta loop. Without a fix, long-tail fights flap out of cache for minutes → intermittent unknown-leg declines on exactly the cards the scraper exists for. **Fix: re-merge hook after every MMA TOA fetch.**
7. **Live in-play for soccer/MMA** — order-tracker.js:4143 routes non-LIVE_ODDS_API_SPORTS live refreshes to fetchOddsForSport, i.e. SharpAPI today. Accept the display-only loss; gate refreshLiveOdds to skip flipped sports outside LIVE_ODDS_API_SPORTS so the dead path doesn't fire.
8. **TOA becomes a single point of failure — and the quota alarm only covers one of the two failure modes.** Quota exhaustion (the 2026-05-12 drought): **non-optional alarm** at 80% of monthly quota pro-rated by day-of-billing-cycle (headers already logged at :1759-1763). Service outage: post-consolidation, a TOA outage darks **the whole book** within ~3–5 min as every sport's stale gate trips — the scrapers cannot hold team lines up (DK supplement is fill-only, and nothing bumps `fetchedAt` on failed fetches). Failure mode is fail-closed (lost volume, no bad quotes — the ride-the-stale-quote confirm channel is bounded at pricer.js:3791-3812), which is acceptable, but say it plainly: consolidation converts "TOA outage = props/NCAAB dark" into "TOA outage = everything dark." **Fix: a TOA-health circuit breaker (~20 lines) that auto-pauses RFQ handling on consecutive fetch failures instead of burning declines.**

- **Delta-loop skip of `TOA_PRIMARY_SPORTS` — live from the very first flip** (index.js:433-439); full deletion only after the last sport flips.
- 30s TOA team-line loop, gated on the same env (cadence/math below).

- **Source tagging on cache rows at fetch time** — Sharp and the TOA supplement both emit untagged 'pinnacle'/'draftkings'/'fanduel' keys and getBookPairs is last-write-wins by (book|line) (:2359-2370), so Sharp-vs-TOA fair probs CANNOT be computed from today's cache. Tagging is a prerequisite for the shadow gate, not a nice-to-have.
- Shadow logger reading the tags, computing fair probs **through the production fetchFromTheOddsApi parse** (post-parity-port, so the gate sees real flip behavior including the floor/filter), exposed at `/shadow-fair-deltas`.

**Week 0b — pricing-parity port (a real pricing change; own commit, own observation window):** port the ≥0.50 Pinnacle floor + filterSharpBooks into the TOA parse. Ships before the shadow week so the shadow compares post-parity behavior. **The bookmaker-list widening does NOT ship here** — see post-flip step below. (Note when it does ship: `ODDS_API_BOOKMAKERS` at :170 AND fetchPinnacleRows' hardcoded 3 books at :1749 both need touching.)

**Week 1 — shadow week (both subscriptions live; budget one ~$500 overlap month):** flip nothing. **Gate: per-sport mean |Δfair| < 0.5pp and p95 < 1.5pp on h2h/spreads/totals.** Coverage caveat: PINNACLE_SPORT_MAP (:1686-1714) has no generic soccer and no tennis, so soccer/WC — the riskiest sport — has zero TOA rows in cache today. **Budget dedicated shadow fetches for it: hourly full-odds pulls for `soccer_fifa_world_cup` + the curated club keys ≈ 7 keys × 3 credits × 24 ≈ 500 credits/day — trivial.** Any sport failing the gate gets investigated before its flip.

**Weeks 2–3 — flip one sport at a time via env, 48h soak each:** NBA → NHL → MMA → soccer/WC → **MLB last** (F5 + pitchers + tightest stale gate). Expectations per flip: a Railway env change **restarts the process and wipes in-memory caches** (oddsCache, lineupCache, closingLinesCache, alt-line warm) — every soak starts with a cold-start decline burst that must NOT be read as a flip regression; judge the soak from hour ~1 onward. Watch `/confirm-activity`, decline-reason mix, per-sport fill rate.

**Fast-refresh cadence (decided, not "or"):** all flipped LEAGUE_MAP team-line keys ride a **30s TOA loop** — the 150s FAST_REFRESH cadence is disqualified by the 3-min stale gates (config.js:530 for MLB; isEventStalePreGame's ≤3-min-within-10-min-of-start applies to ALL sports, :6660-6668), which today only pass because the 30s delta loop bumps `fetchedAt` (:6843-6851). Soccer gets a **curated subset**: only keys with currently-registered PX lines (cap ~6) at 60s; all other discovered soccer keys stay on the main refresh cycle. Math: NBA+NHL+MLB (3 keys × 3 credits) + MMA (1 credit, h2h-only) + WC (3) = 13 credits × 2,880 ticks/day ≈ 37K/day, plus curated soccer 6 × 3 × 1,440 ≈ 26K/day → **~63K/day ≈ 1.9M/mo**, matching the headline estimate. The 17–23M winter projection gets re-run with this cadence sheet as a **hard pre-cancel gate**; if it breaches, the documented lever is the alt-line book-breadth trim (kept decoupled, §5).

**Post-flip, pre-cancel (own change window):** widen the TOA bulk bookmaker list 3 → 8–10 (free per-10 billing; recovers de-vig depth lost with Caesars/BetMGM). This is a pricing-input change — it ships alone, after the vig filter is live and all flips have soaked, so a pricing regression can never be confused with a routing regression.

**Rollback:** routing rollback = env flip back + restart — minutes. Week-0 structural changes roll back by git revert + redeploy — they run in production from the week-0 deploy while everything still nominally routes to Sharp, so watch that deploy like any other. **Keep SharpAPI billing alive until every sport has run ≥1 clean week on TOA** (pay the one overlap month — after cancellation, rollback degrades to "re-subscribe and hope"). Then cancel.

**Post-cancel:** re-probe TOA burn (free `/sports` call) after 1 week and again at first winter slate; confirm the billing-anchor-corrected burn + soccer-inclusive winter projection still clears the 25M plan.

## 5. Explicitly NOT Migrating (and why)

- **DataGolf (golf model pricing)** — separate key, separate product (model probabilities, not book odds). Out of scope by definition.
- **DK / Bovada / BetOnline Puppeteer scrapers** — free, and load-bearing backstops post-Sharp (MMA cards via the re-merge hook, WC props fallback, NBA-H1/team-total/NHL-period alts, golf). Keep all. Known limit, stated honestly: the DK game-line supplement is fill-only — it enriches cached events but cannot create them, so it backstops thin markets, not dark ones (tennis overnight, TOA outage).
- **ESPN scores** — free and already primary for settlement; TOA `/scores` stays the fallback only.
- **Alt-line book breadth (17 books)** — do NOT trim from 17→10 as part of this migration. It would halve the biggest credit item (~130K/day) but changes pricing inputs; the 25M plan doesn't need the savings. It is the designated relief valve if the winter re-projection surprises high — decoupled from the cutover so a pricing regression can't be confused with a routing regression.
- **Prop pipeline, alt-line warm loops, pin-verify, closing lines** — already TOA; touching them adds risk for zero savings. (Accepted nit: closing-line snapshots for flipped sports go from delta-loop-fresh to 30s-loop-fresh — effectively unchanged at the chosen cadence.)
- **The 25M TOA plan itself** — do not downgrade to the public 15M tier even if the invoice tempts you; winter peak (17–23M, pending the soccer-inclusive re-run) makes 15M a coin flip, and quota exhaustion has already caused one fill drought.
