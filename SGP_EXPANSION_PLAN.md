# Same-Game Multi-Prop SGP Roadmap — Staged, Fail-Closed, Calibration-Gated (rev. 2)

**The one-paragraph answer:** We can open same-game multi-prop SGPs safely because the $50 prop cap makes every pricing error *bounded* (dollar cost per maxed ticket = $50/(1−q) per pp of joint-probability error — ranging ~$0.51/pp at longshot q≈0.025 to ~$1.00/pp at q≈0.50, so every class's residual is quoted in pp **at that class's q**, never a one-size unit), and because the combo classes differ enormously in tractability: logically-nested pairs are priced **exactly** with zero model risk, cross-team game-environment pairs have a structurally narrow dependence band we price **additively at the band top** (the flat multiplicative boost in rev. 1 undercovered low-probability pairs — MLB HR+HR, the #1 demand shape — by >10×; fixed), and only the genuinely hard classes (same-player two-stat, linked same-team pairs) need real calibration — which comes from a book-SGP oracle and box scores, never from our own fills (441 joint observations all-time across ALL classes, vs per-class needs of ~400–3,300 depending on estimator and margins — insufficient under every reading). The order of operations: harden the holes the red-team found that the expansion would weaponize (confirm-path failure handling, prop staleness + refresh cadence, confirm-time prop cap, game-script aggregation + class stop-loss, player identity), then open classes strictly in order of pricing certainty, each behind an env-gated allowlist key, with **price-space (CLV) monitors as the primary tripwire** — settled-ticket hit-rate gates are gross-blunder detectors only and are statistically blind exactly where sharps will attack (longshot classes).

## Design invariants (apply to every stage)

1. **Confirm-path symmetry.** `validateForConfirmation` repricing carries no opts (`pricer.js:3352-3398`). Every new fair-prob adjustment MUST re-derive from leg shape inside `priceParlay` (the `kprop_ml` pattern at `pricer.js:1550-1568`), and MUST be mirrored into `sgpFairMultiplier` (`pricer.js:1733-1736`) and the per-leg alignment (`pricer.js:1827-1829`). Opts-driven fair boosts = phantom drift = every fill rejected. **Corollary (calibration versioning):** any table-driven parameter (Stage 3+) is stamped by version into quote meta, and confirms reprice against the SAME version — the drift threshold is **3%** (`config.js:551`, not 5%), so a re-fit or deploy between quote and confirm would otherwise mass-reject every in-flight SGP (re-creating the 2026-05-12 walk-away regression) or tempt a drift exemption that re-opens confirm asymmetry. Drift fires on leg-level fair moves only.
2. **Env-gated kill switches.** New combos go in `SGP_ALLOWED_COMBOS` semantics (operator-disableable), NOT hard auto-included like `kprop_ml`/`kprop_kprop` at `pricer.js:3088`. One Railway env edit kills any stage.
3. **Fail closed, with one calibrated exception.** No price source → decline. Structural check fails → decline. Unresolved player identity → decline. The confirm-time reprice-failure path is **cause-and-time-bounded**, not blanket (see Stage 0 item 1) — a bare reject-on-failure re-imports the 2026-05-12 fill-drought the current fail-open was built to fix.
4. **Mirror before model.** Where a book posts the joint (or a nested equivalent), inherit their margin **by using their number as-is** — never subtract a "haircut" that hands the margin back (see Stage 3); model only where the dependence interval is provably narrow, and price at the conservative end of it.
5. **Assume the pricing grid is public.** RFQs are free; the quote-vs-silence bit plus returned odds ARE an oracle regardless of what we log or display (declines themselves are silent — no decline detail ever reaches PX; verified `websocket.js:1425-1441`). Every opened class must be safe under total transparency: a sharp who fully reverse-engineers our formula must still face non-positive EV. Exact pricing (Stage 1), band-top pricing (Stage 2), and pure mirroring (Stage 5) satisfy this by construction; calibrated classes (3–4) satisfy it via conservative-side parameters + hard caps.
6. **One correlation language.** Two scales exist and every number in this plan is labeled: **φ-scale** = Pearson correlation of the binary outcomes (joint lift is *additive*: Δjoint = φ·√(p₁q₁p₂q₂)); **latent-scale** = Gaussian-copula/tetrachoric ρ (lift = copula density × ρ). Stage 2's band is φ-scale; Stage 4's copula is latent-scale fitted tetrachorically. Box-score φ estimates feeding a copula formula MUST pass through the threshold conversion — mixing scales is itself a mispricing (φ=0.15 at p=0.5 lifts 3.75pp; latent ρ=0.15 lifts ~2.4pp).
7. **PX acceptance is a launch risk, monitored from the first quote of every class.** PX has rejected multiplicative fair-prob corrections on team-line SGPs with "invalid estimated prices" (`config.js:815-834`); `kprop_ml`'s 0.15 boost passes today, so prop combos appear to be treated differently — but each new class raises fair prob above the naive product (Stage 1 by ~28%!) and a silent rejection pattern would make validation gates unreachable while looking like zero demand. Per-class PX-rejection counters ship with the class, day one. If PX rejects our corrected price, the class **stays declined** — we never fall back to the longer (exploitable) price.
8. **Demand truth:** size on deduped declines and competitor fills, not raw decline counts. Working numbers — competitors filled ~$47K/day of multi-prop SGPs over the last 10.7 days; ~29% of declined shapes unique (≈3.5× bot inflation) — are **verified against the DB as a Stage 0 line item** before they set opening order or pricing parameters. Targets: MLB HR+HR / hits+hits, NBA pts/reb/ast/threes 2–3-player stacks, prop+ML/spread/total.

## Stage 0 — Harden + instrument (no new quoting). SHIP FIRST.

**WHAT ships** (all in-repo, CommonJS idioms):

That was the right fix for its cause (index churn: a leg flapping below `minBooks` at refresh walked us away from 3 won auctions in 44s on 2026-05-12), but as written it's also a ride-the-stale-quote channel for any leg type. Replace with: accept on original quote ONLY when (a) `priceParlay._lastFailure` indicates transient index churn on a leg that priced at quote time, AND (b) quote age is within the staleness budget for its leg types (props: `STALE_PROP_SECONDS`; team: `stalePriceMinutes`). Otherwise reject. This closes the channel for team-line parlays too, without re-importing the fill-drought. **Companion:** shorten offer validity for prop-containing parlays — `offerValidSeconds` is 60 (`config.js:552`), which lets a bettor sit on any prop quote for a minute regardless of staleness gates; add `OFFER_VALID_SECONDS_PROP` default **30**.
2. **Confirm-time prop cap** — `websocket.js:1742-1748` only checks `maxRiskPerParlay` ($500); add `parlayHasProp → maxRiskPerParlayWithProp` ($50). PX has documentedly ignored offer-level `max_risk` before; this is the only enforcement we control.

4. **Per-game prop exposure caps + per-class stop-loss** — the single biggest structural gap (Attack 3B), now script-aware: `checkGameExposure`/`recalcNetExposure` (`order-tracker.js:3482-3749`) net by `(market):(selection)`, so N ≤$50 tickets on N *different* players long the same game script aggregate unbounded. A two-bucket `(game, direction)` cap is evadable — 'A blows out B' pays A-player overs AND B-player unders simultaneously, so an attacker fills both buckets with one script. Ship three layers: (a) per-`(pxEventId, team, side)` cap (start $300, env `MAX_PROP_TEAM_SIDE_RISK`); (b) **total** prop risk per game regardless of direction (start $600, env `MAX_PROP_RISK_PER_GAME`) — the blunt bound no script mapping can evade; (c) **per-combo-class slate-wide stop-loss** — daily filled-risk cap per class plus auto-dark (remove the class's `SGP_ALLOWED_COMBOS` behavior at runtime) on rolling-week realized-P&L breach, because a *systematic* class mispricing leaks across every game on every slate (15-game MLB slates × a season ≈ $40–50K at $18/game) and per-game caps don't bound it. Re-check all caps at confirm (mirror the team-cooldown pattern at `websocket.js:1778-1814`), and add the confirm-time **per-player** exposure re-check (`order-tracker.js:3453-3458` admits the gap).
5. **Probe detection that works without creator_id.** Declines are silent — no detail string reaches PX, so there is no wire leak to genericize; the real probing surface is quote-vs-silence plus returned odds (invariant 5), and the real attribution problem is that PX omits `creator_id` on live RFQs (the documented creator-blocklist gap — the live creator lookup only happens at confirm via the 800ms-bounded `fetchOrderByUuid`). So: detect probes **behaviorally** on the decline side (shape-grid fingerprints — line-ladder sweeps, pair-grid scans, RFQ timing patterns), capture creator at confirm time and backfill the association, and add an optional fail-closed knob declining prop-SGP confirms with null creator. Keep verbose decline detail in logs/db/dashboard — it only ever went there.
6. **Enrich `sgp_audit`, scoped for cost** (verified: ~34.8M rows since 2026-05-17, ~1.4M/day). Do NOT enrich the whole firehose: add `creator_id` (expect null-dominant per item 5) and a content hash for dedup-aware demand counting everywhere; add per-leg book odds (`pinnacleOdds/draftkingsOdds/fanduelOdds`, `propSource`, `propFetchedAt`, `booksWithBothSides`) **only for the SGP-shaped decline subset** (prop-correlation + 'SGP not allowed' reasons), read synchronously from the in-memory odds cache (no awaits in the decline path), sampled if volume demands. Extend shadow-logging to `prop_correlation_same_player` (`pricer.js:2748-2752`), `prop_correlation_same_pitcher`, and `'SGP not allowed'`. Add a retention/rollup policy.
7. **Persist closing lines** — `closingLinesCache` (`odds-feed.js:153, 7015`) is in-memory, wiped per deploy; create a `closing_lines` table and extend capture to prop markets. Prop closing capture hits TOA per-event prop endpoints (game-line capture reads the existing cache for free; props won't) — capture only registered props on games with quoting activity, and budget the quota alongside item 3. Prerequisite for the **filled-vs-quoted CLV monitor** (the master adverse-selection detector) and for grading every later stage.

Plus: per-creator (and per-fingerprint, per item 5) volume/fill-rate/shape-diversity; count of confirm reprice-failure accepts by creator; per-game cap utilization; **per-class PX 'invalid estimated prices' rejection counters** (invariant 7). Dashboard + `/sgp-stats` (already aggregates by `meta.sgpCombo`).
9. **Cheap fixes:** (a) verify `_normPlayer` (`pricer.js:2724`) and `playerKeyForLeg` agree on accented/nickname names — necessary but NOT sufficient for identity (the real fix is the Stage 2 roster service); (b) **remove `kprop_kprop`'s `skipSgpVig` now** — opposing-pitcher Ks share umpire zone, park K-factor, and weather, the same common factor Stage 2 charges for; leaving a known-looser carve-out live through Stages 1–2 hands attackers a preferred template (it gets folded into the Stage 2 class when that ships); (c) on 3+ same-game stacks: `classifySgpCombo` returns null for `entries.length !== 2` (`pricer.js:3071`) and null combos hard-decline ('SGP not allowed', `pricer.js:3134-3140`) — 3+ stacks are **already closed by decline** and the `by3Plus` pricing path (`pricer.js:1513-1523`) is unreachable for new quotes; bumping its default 1.15→1.6 is optional defense-in-depth in case classification ever changes, not a hole-close.
10. **Verify the demand numbers** ($47K/day competitor multi-prop fills, dedup rate, 441 joint observations) with direct paginated DB queries before they set opening order or Stage 2 parameters.

**PRICING RULE:** none — no new quoting.
**GATE to Stage 1:** items 1–8 deployed; closing-line persistence accumulating ≥3 days; caps and confirm handling verified by replaying recent confirms in a script; TOA quota headroom confirmed after the cadence cut; demand numbers verified.

## Stage 1 — Nested implication pairs: exact pricing, zero dependence risk

The single most exploitable hole if ever priced naively, and free margin when priced correctly: P(goal)=0.45 ∧ P(SoT1+)=0.78 priced as a product — at the actual prop-SGP vig schedule (vigPropFloor 3% × sgpVigMultiplier 2 = 6%/leg) — quotes ≈**+154** on a **+116** truth, a standing ≈**+14%** bettor ROI gift; priced exactly it's ≈−3% bettor EV with zero model risk.

**WHAT ships:**
- Static implication table (~12 entries), keyed on the **full tuple `(stat_a, side_a, stat_b, side_b, line-relation)`** — sides are load-bearing: K-Over 6.5 + K-Under 4.5 is disjoint, hits-Over + HR-NO is negatively linked; only matching-side, implication-satisfying tuples qualify, and everything else stays under the existing same-player block (the permanently-blocked negative class must not re-enter through this carve-out). Entries: soccer GS⇒SoT1+, SoT2+⇒SoT1+; NHL assist⇒point, 2pt⇒1pt; MLB HR⇒hit, HR⇒RBI, HR⇒TB(line≤3.5), K-ladder; NBA pts-ladder, m-threes⇒pts(line≤3m−0.5).
- Relax `prop_correlation_same_player` (`pricer.js:2738-2755`) ONLY for table-whitelisted tuples; widen the pre-pass exemption predicate (`pricer.js:2798`); new `classifySgpCombo` key `prop_nested` (env-gated via `SGP_ALLOWED_COMBOS`); structural check in the rule-(d) block (`pricer.js:2955-3035`).
- Pricing re-derives from leg shape in `priceParlay` (invariant 1): detect the nested pair, collapse to the strong leg.
- **Settlement-definition verification per pair AND per grading-window** before enabling each: does PX grade a goal as an SoT (Opta convention), an assist as a point, what happens when one leg voids — and critically, **extra-time windows**: WC group games have no ET, so verification against group-stage graded history passes trivially and then knockout ET grading (does PX's 'To Score a Goal' include ET while SoT props are 90'-only?) can break GS⇒SoT1+ exactly when stakes peak. Any pair unverifiable for a grading window stays dark for that window.
- **PX acceptance monitor from quote one** (invariant 7): collapsing to the strong leg raises fair prob ~28% above the naive product — exactly the upward-correction shape PX rejected on team lines. If PX rejects, the class stays declined.

**PRICING RULE:** `p* = p_strong` exactly. Quote `q = p_strong × (1 + vigPropFloor)`; where the strong leg is book-mirrored today (WC props), `q = mirror(strong leg)` — a single-leg price wearing a parlay costume.
**GATE to Stage 2:** 50–100 settled nested tickets with realized win rate inside the binomial CI of the strong leg's fair prob — stated honestly, this is a ±10–14pp gross-blunder check (it catches settlement-definition catastrophes, not vig calibration), which is sufficient *here only* because the pricing is arithmetic; zero settlement-definition surprises; zero PX-rejection anomalies; all Stage-0 monitors green.
**CALIBRATION DEPENDENCE:** none. This is arithmetic, not statistics.
**RESIDUAL RISK:** settlement-definition mismatch + void-leg interaction only — worst case $50/ticket, eliminated by the per-window verification; expected ≈ $0. Positive selection: these requests are mostly bettor confusion.
**EFFORT:** 2–3 days code + 1 day settlement verification.
**DEFENSES:** closes the canonical SGP exploit (nested-pair naive product) preemptively, and is safe under a public grid (invariant 5) by construction. Same-player rule stays in force for everything outside the table.

## Stage 2 — Cross-team game-environment pairs: additive band-top pricing

**PREREQUISITE — player-identity/roster service (the real long pole).** The structural check needs to prove two things the codebase currently cannot: that the legs are on **opposite teams**, and that they are **different humans**. `lineupCache` (`odds-feed.js:119-136`) tracks only MLB starting pitchers and NHL goalies; there is no player→team resolution anywhere for NBA players or MLB hitters (for props, `lineInfo.teamName` is the *player's* name, `line-manager.js:1141`), and name-keyed identity is wrong in both directions (one human under variant names across PX/TOA feeds slides the most-correlated, permanently-blocked same-player class through as 'different players'; multiple active players share normalized names, e.g. Luis García). Build a per-sport roster source producing resolved `(player_id, team)` per prop leg; **decline on unresolved player identity, not just unresolved team**. Without this, fail-closed correctly declines essentially every Stage-2 candidate — only `kprop_kprop` works today, and only because the same-pitcher pre-pass makes opposing-pitcher true by construction. Effort: ~1 week, and it is the Stage-2 schedule.

**WHAT ships:** 2-prop, different players (resolved identity), **opposite teams**, same game (NBA pts-over A-team + pts-over B-team; MLB cross-team HR+HR / hits+hits — the #1 declined-and-competitor-filled shape). New combo key `prop_prop_xteam`, env-gated. `kprop_kprop` reclassified into this class (same pricing, same vig — its independence + zero-SGP-vig special status ends here; Stage 0 already removed the vig suppression).

**PRICING RULE — additive, at the band top (φ-scale, invariant 6):**
`q_fair = min( p₁·p₂ + φ_max·√(p₁q₁p₂q₂), min(p₁,p₂) )`, with `φ_max = 0.15` (env knob `sgpPropXTeamPhiMax`), then standard prop SGP vig `× Π(1+v_leg)` (6%/leg).
Why additive and why band-top: correlation lift is additive in probability, so a flat multiplicative boost is structurally mismatched — 8% on the product covers φ≤0.08 at p=0.5 but only φ≤0.009 at p=0.10, i.e., it failed worst exactly on the longshot MLB binaries that dominate demand (at p₁=p₂=0.10, φ=0.15 lifts the joint 1.0pp→2.35pp, a +135% lift, and a naive-product quote there hands ~+90–100% ROI to anyone selecting high-common-factor pairs — Coors/wind-out HR+HR). And because selection pins realized dependence at the **top** of the band (sharps request the linked pairs; any mispriced joint can be middled against the leg markets risk-free and maxed to the cap — the Stage-5 selection argument applies equally here), the band top is the no-regret price: a fully-informed counterparty faces ≤0 EV anywhere in φ∈[0, 0.15] (invariant 5). The cost of band-top pricing is fill rate on genuinely-independent pairs, not EV.
Note on vig at low q: 6%/leg is worth ~0.3pp ≈ $0.15/maxed ticket at q≈0.025 — **vig is not the safety margin for longshot binaries; the φ_max charge is.** Restate per-class margin in pp, never in dollars-at-q=0.35.

**GATE to Stage 3:** price-space first — odds-bucketed, EV-weighted filled-vs-quoted CLV gap < 1.5pp per bucket with the CUSUM tripwire armed (Stage 0 item 8); the betslip-oracle recon spike's DK pair quotes sanity-check φ_max for MLB binaries before launch and continuously after; 200 settled tickets per sport retained as a gross-blunder backstop only (power stated: detects ~6pp at q≈0.27, nothing at q≈0.01); per-game cap and class stop-loss utilization reviewed; PX-rejection counter clean (invariant 7 — an additive fair-prob lift is still an upward correction vs PX's model; if PX rejects, the class stays declined).
**CALIBRATION DEPENDENCE:** none to launch (the band is structural: pace/park/weather common factor bounds φ well under 0.15 for cross-team binaries); oracle quotes refine φ_max later, only ever downward with evidence.
**RESIDUAL RISK:** φ exceeding 0.15 structurally (extreme park/weather days): true φ=0.25 on a p=0.10 pair = 0.9pp miss vs our band-top fair ≈ **$0.46/maxed ticket** at that class's q≈0.025 ($/pp ≈ $0.51) — bounded per game by the Stage-0 caps and per slate by the class stop-loss. Typical (φ<0.15) flow is overcharged, by design.
**EFFORT:** ~1–1.5 weeks: roster/identity service (~1 week) + the pricing diff (predicate widening, structural check, classify key, additive lift site + `sgpFairMultiplier` mirror, knob — ~2 days).
**DEFENSES:** 1A closed for this class under total transparency (band-top ≥ plausible φ); 3B load-bearing via Stage-0 caps; 5E closed by resolved-identity requirement. Same-team pairs still declined; shadow log keeps measuring them.

## Stage 3 — Book-oracle calibration infra + prop+ML generalization + same-team different-player binaries

The stage where the **book-SGP oracle** gets built — the calibration engine every later stage reuses. NOT in the RFQ hot path (rejected: 1–3s warm Puppeteer round-trips, Akamai bans, the 2026-06-10 "no blocking awaits" lesson).

**WHAT ships:**
1. **Nightly batch oracle — new betslip automation, not a scraper extension.** DK SGP-builder *joint* prices are not in the passively-intercepted market-list XHRs the existing WC infra reads (`services/dk-scraper.js`, `scripts/dk-wc-props.js`) — they require active slip automation: add 2+ selections, read the combined quote from DK's slip-pricing endpoint (itself Akamai-gated). FanDuel is a separate stack with zero existing code. Plan: DK-only first; a **feasibility spike this week** (THIS WEEK item 4) produces go/no-go evidence before the build is scheduled. Per combo class, 10–30 DK quotes/night across (p₁,p₂) grid cells and line ladders. Until FD exists, σ_ρ comes from week-over-week DK self-disagreement plus DK-vs-box-score residuals. Identification caveats stated up front: DK's hold is NOT combo-invariant (their engine pads more-correlated pairs — that's their armor) and the builder blocks many correlated combos outright (especially same-player), so nested/cross-game hold-anchoring yields a *lower bound* on correlated-pair hold and the grid is unobservable exactly where dependence is highest. Consequences: extracted ρ_book is treated as conservatively-biased-unknown and used from the safe side; **where the builder blocks a combo, we don't quote it either** — a book refusing to price a joint is information. Nightly cadence cannot track day-scale park/weather/pace swings; that is handled by pricing at the conservative end of the measured band, not by pretending to track them. Weekly re-fit lands in a `sgp_combo_calibration` table, **version-stamped per invariant 1's corollary** — quotes carry the version, confirms reprice against the same version.
2. **Prop + same-team ML/total, generalized from kprop_ml**: NHL SOG-over+ML, NBA star pts-over+ML, MLB hitter TB-over+ML, soccer GS+DNB. Per-(sport, propType) boost map (replacing the single global `sgpPropMlCorrBoost`), seeded at the conservative end (~0.12–0.15), near-primary lines only (existing ±2 alt-distance gate — kills the non-monotone blowout channel at high lines), Fréchet cap.
3. **Same-team different-player binaries** (MLB HR+HR / hits+hits same team — the rest of the #1 demand shape): additive band-top like Stage 2 (`q_fair = p₁p₂ + φ_max·√(p₁q₁p₂q₂)`, Fréchet-capped) with φ_max seeded from the oracle (shared park/pitcher driver, expected φ 0.05–0.20); enabled per-sport only once the oracle grid confirms the band, and φ_max sits at the **top** of the confirmed band.

**PRICING RULE:** joint from `ρ̂ = ρ_book` **as-is — no haircut.** If books overcharge correlation, using their number unchanged is what inherits their margin; subtracting a haircut moves the price toward (or past) truth, hands the margin back, and *lengthens* odds on correlated stacks — the unsafe direction, contradicting Stage 4's ρ_hi = ρ̂ + 2σ. It would also make us best-price on every correlated combo precisely when the originating book says the combo is worth more — a line-shopping selection magnet. Any future competitiveness concession requires an explicit per-class EV budget charged in the residual-risk accounting. Margin comes from the vig structure. Fréchet cap always.
**GATE to Stage 4:** oracle grid running ≥2 weeks with week-over-week ρ̂ drift < 0.03 per class; σ_ρ measured per class (cross-source once available); 200 settled prop+ML tickets per sport as blunder backstop, price-space CLV as the real gate; PX-rejection counters reviewed per class.
**CALIBRATION DEPENDENCE:** oracle quotes pin **ρ_book** quickly (a night's ladder gives sampling precision ±0.01–0.02), but accuracy on **ρ_true** is floored at the book-bias level — cross-source disagreement ~±0.05–0.07 — regardless of scrape volume; that floor is the binding uncertainty and the band-top/conservative-side rules price it. (The rev-1 "≈500× more sample-efficient" framing conflated this precision with accuracy and is withdrawn; the operative claim is simply that own settles can never calibrate — 441 joint observations all-time across all classes vs per-class needs of ~400 at best — and the oracle can.)
**RESIDUAL RISK:** ρ_true uncertainty ±0.07 at the floor → ~0.6–1.0pp on mid-q combos ≈ **$0.5–0.9/maxed ticket at 2σ**, covered by the SGP vig at mid-q and by conservative-side seeding at low q; blowout non-monotonicity bounded by the line gate; aggregate bounded by Stage-0 caps + class stop-loss. Operational: betslip-automation fragility (new risk class beyond the documented subcategory-ID churn — budget maintenance).
**EFFORT:** 2–3 weeks for the betslip oracle + fit pipeline (was understated at ~1 week); 2–3 days per-sport pricing code (map lookups on the existing boost path).
**DEFENSES:** closes 1A for prop+team-line (per-combo measured factor replaces global guess); the oracle continuously audits Stages 1–2 prices against book joints.

## Stage 4 — NBA/WNBA same-player two-stat (pts+reb, pts+ast) — buffered copula

The first stage with genuine model risk; everything before it built the measurement apparatus.

**WHAT ships:** lift `prop_correlation_same_player` for exactly two whitelisted stat pairs (NOT reb+ast — lowest signal, highest pooled σ; never 3 stats). Box-score calibration pipeline: one NBA season ≈ 10K player-games per class cell → latent-ρ (tetrachoric) fits with stated assumptions (player-game clustering inflates the asymptotic SE; we budget δρ≈0.06/season, ±0.04 pooled over two — deliberately fatter than the i.i.d. asymptotic ~0.016 to absorb clustering and pooling heterogeneity). **Threshold-dependence is first-class, not an afterthought:** dependence is driven by the shared minutes/OT/blowout factor and grows in the tails, and a Gaussian copula fit at medians has zero tail dependence — so ρ is fitted **per line-distance bucket**, the **±2 alt-distance line gate is extended to this class explicitly** (the attack is requesting star pairs only at the highest carried lines), and the book PRA/PR ladder bound is promoted from cross-check to **hard cap with decline-when-absent**: joint ≤ book P(P+R > a+b+0.5) — pts>a and reb>b at half-lines force P+R ≥ a+b+1, so a+b+0.5 is the tight exact ceiling (a full point tighter than the rev-1 a+b−0.5 bound). Beta-Binomial per-player shrinkage on the conditional c=P(leg2|leg1), prior strength m=150 player-games.

**PRICING RULE:** `ρ_hi = ρ̂_bucket + 2σ_ρ` → `p_Q = min(C_{ρ_hi}(p₁,p₂), PRA-ladder cap, min(p₁,p₂))` → `q = p_Q × (1+v_sgp)`, `v_sgp ≥ 8%` effective. Stated honestly: the vig here is **padding on top of an already-2σ price** — the effective buffer is ~4σ, a deliberate double-charge whose cost is fill-rate/competitiveness, accepted because this is the first class where the model itself (single-ρ copula vs minutes-script tail dependence) can be structurally wrong. Class ρ̂ expectations (latent scale): pts–reb 0.20–0.35, pts–ast 0.15–0.30. All box-score φ estimates pass the threshold conversion before entering the copula (invariant 6).
**GATE (ongoing):** weekly price-space monitor (quoted joint vs oracle joint and vs PRA-ladder implied ceiling) odds-bucketed with the CUSUM tripwire; realized-vs-predicted hit-rate as blunder backstop; monthly tri-validation (box scores / oracle / settled tickets).
**CALIBRATION DEPENDENCE:** 2 seasons box scores per class, per line-distance bucket (ρ̂±0.04 at medians, wider in tail buckets — priced via the per-bucket 2σ) + book PRA ladder back-outs BEFORE launch; settled tickets for validation cycles AFTER.
**RESIDUAL RISK:** minutes-script tail dependence beyond a single ρ: bounded by the per-bucket fit + 2σ buffer + PRA hard cap + Fréchet cap ≈ **$1.20/maxed ticket at 2σ** at this class's q, aggregate bounded by player caps + Stage-0 game caps + class stop-loss.
**EFFORT:** 1–2 weeks (box-score ingestion is the long pole; pricing code reuses the Stage 2/3 path).

## Stage 5 — Soccer same-team cross-player pairs (WC): book-mirror ONLY

**WHAT ships:** A-goalscorer + B-goalscorer / A-assist + B-goal, quoted **only** when a batch-collected DK SGP pair quote for that exact pair exists and passes a lineup-aware freshness gate. **Dependency honesty:** the service-side WC scraper→pricer pipeline was deleted when WC props moved to TOA (commit c6eada3); what remains is the standalone `scripts/dk-wc-props.js` and a dormant lookup path (`line-manager.js:1641-1648`) — and per-pair SGP joints are not in the passively-intercepted market XHRs anyway. This stage therefore **depends on Stage 3's betslip automation** (same machinery pointed at WC pairs) plus new ingestion plumbing, and is sequenced after it. Pair-specific dependence (does A actually feed B?) + requested-pair selection make this class permanently un-modelable for us — mirror or decline.
**FRESHNESS (replaces the rev-1 12h rule, which contradicted our own staleness logic by two orders of magnitude):** pair quote ≤6h old outside T−2h of kickoff, **≤60 min inside T−2h**; ALL pair quotes for a match are invalidated the moment the lineup trigger fires (Stage 0 item 3's mechanism — official lineups drop ~60–75 min pre-kickoff, and a benched scorer reprices every teammate's GS/assist probability); each leg must additionally pass the standard per-leg prop staleness gate. No fresh quote → decline. Fail closed, no fallback.
**PRICING RULE:** `q = q_DK` at par — sweetener 0 for this class (undercutting DK makes us best-price exactly when DK is wrong, concentrating adverse selection on their mistakes).
**RESIDUAL RISK:** *bounded by DK's joint-pricing accuracy, not zero* — we inherit their errors; mitigated by par pricing (no systematic undercut), the lineup-trigger invalidation, and the Stage-0 caps. Operational: betslip-automation fragility (shared with Stage 3).
**EFFORT:** ~1 week on top of the Stage 3 oracle infra.

## Permanently blocked (by design, not by backlog)

- **All negatively-correlated combos** (teammates competing for rebounds/touches, over+under structures): state-dependent dependence sign a single ρ can't represent, longshot odds-space leverage, scissor-portfolio risk, trivial revenue at the cap. The RFQ re-frame: the danger isn't "sharps take the other side" — it's a positive lift leaking onto a negative pair and *lengthening* the requested side's odds. The Stage-1 side-keyed table is the enforcement edge: non-matching-side same-player tuples never qualify.
- **3+ prop legs on one game** — already hard-declined today (`classifySgpCombo` null for ≠2 legs); revisit only after Stage 4 has months of green tripwire history.
- reb+ast same player; tennis SGPs; any combo DK's own builder refuses to price; anything whose only price source would be our own uncalibrated guess.
